Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

RallyPad is a scoreboard app made by Joshua Ruis. This policy covers the RallyPad apps for iOS and Android.

We don't collect data

RallyPad has no account, no login, no backend server, no analytics, and no ads. We don't collect, store, share, or sell any personal information, and nothing you enter in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your device

Scores, match history, team names, and your preferred sport are saved only in the app's storage on your device. You can delete all of it at any time from Settings → Clear all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Donations

Where the app offers a donation option, tapping it opens a Stripe Checkout page in your browser. Stripe processes the payment under its own privacy policy. RallyPad never sees or stores your card number or payment details.

Children

RallyPad doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
