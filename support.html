FAQ

Do I need an account? No. Everything stays on your phone.

How do I undo a point? Tap Undo on the scoreboard. It removes the last scoring event.

How do I change the default sport? Tap the sport chip at the top of the scoreboard, or go to Settings → Change sport.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Clear all data. This removes all matches, names, and settings from the device.

Will my matches move to a new phone? Not yet. Data is stored only on the device.
